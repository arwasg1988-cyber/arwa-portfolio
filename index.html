About

I design with the full product in mind.

My path into product design was not a straight line—and that became one of my strongest advantages.

I started with a background in Business Administration, which taught me to look beyond the interface and understand priorities, operations, and the reason a product needs to exist.

Graphic design trained my eye for visual clarity. UX/UI shifted my focus toward people, behaviour, and solving the right problem. Front-end development then gave me a practical understanding of how design decisions behave in real products.

Today, I bring those perspectives together as a product designer who codes—shaping bilingual digital experiences from early thinking and user flows through polished interfaces and implementation-ready delivery.

What I bring Business awareness, user-centred thinking, and the ability to bridge design with development.
